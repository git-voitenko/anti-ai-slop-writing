# Deep audit

Use this module only for an explicit audit, important public copy, or a stubborn rewrite. Work from strong problems to weak signals.

## P0: meaning and trust

- Unsupported facts, sources, consensus, numbers, quotations, rankings, or causal claims.
- A rewrite that drops a condition, comparator, deadline, option, caveat, or emotional line carried by the source.
- Chat instructions, placeholders, or drafting notes left inside the deliverable.
- An invented personality: fake first person, anecdotes, outrage, doubt, slang, or contrarianism.

Fix these before style. Preserve quoted and attributed material even when it contains an awkward pattern.

## P1: generic machinery

- The opening announces the topic instead of stating the useful point.
- Importance is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- Several stock phrases, decorative adjectives, or vague attributions cluster in one passage.
- Paragraphs repeat the same setup, explanation, example, and conclusion.
- Contrasts reject claims nobody made; lists fall into threes without a content reason.
- The same entity is renamed repeatedly to avoid a normal repetition.
- The ending summarizes, cheers, asks a rhetorical question, and then closes again.

Make the minimum edit that removes the pattern. If the source lacks the detail needed for a concrete rewrite, stay plain or ask; never fill the gap.

## P2: contextual style

- Several consecutive sentences have similar length or openings.
- Dashes, semicolons, fragments, passive voice, bold labels, emoji, or headings are repeated by habit rather than used for a purpose.
- The register is too polished for a chat message or too casual for the document.

These are judgment calls. A single instance is usually harmless. The author's sample and the genre override generic advice.

## Audit loop

1. Read the whole piece and mark only observable problems.
2. Rewrite once, preserving all source claims and distinctive lines.
3. Compare source and rewrite for factual and semantic drift.
4. Re-read the result aloud. Correct surviving clusters without forcing artificial irregularity.
5. Stop after one corrective pass unless the user asks for further iteration.

In audit-only mode, report the span, the pattern, its severity, and a brief fix. Do not claim that a person or model wrote the text, and do not present a detector score as fact.

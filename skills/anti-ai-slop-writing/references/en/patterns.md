# English – module 2: rewrite patterns (before → after)

Standalone module: edit independently of rules.md and banned.md. Sourced from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" (WikiProject AI Cleanup).

### 1. Copula avoidance
**Before:**
> Gallery 825 serves as the exhibition space and boasts over 3,000 square feet.
**After:**
> Gallery 825 is the exhibition space, with 3,000 square feet across four rooms.

### 2. Superficial -ing analyses
**Before:**
> The temple's colors resonate with the region's beauty, symbolizing the bluebonnets and reflecting the community's deep connection to the land.
**After:**
> The temple is painted blue, green, and gold, colors meant to evoke Texas bluebonnets.

### 3. Synonym cycling
**Before:**
> The protagonist faces challenges. The main character overcomes obstacles. The central figure triumphs.
**After:**
> The protagonist faces many challenges but eventually triumphs.

### 4. False ranges
**Before:**
> From the singularity of the Big Bang to the enigmatic dance of dark matter, our journey spans the cosmos.
**After:**
> The book covers the Big Bang, star formation, and current theories about dark matter.

### 5. Generic positive conclusions
**Before:**
> The future looks bright as they continue their journey toward excellence.
**After:**
> (Cut the paragraph. End on the last concrete fact.)
